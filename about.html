About

Built by a Closer and an Engineer.

Caiden

SpurIntel started with a simple observation: great B2B products were struggling to fill their pipeline because manual prospecting doesn't scale and generic automation burns brands. I'd seen it firsthand working alongside my brother Cooper, a top enterprise sales rep at a Fortune Global 500 company who consistently ranked top two in both sales and SDR outreach.

He knew what converted cold outreach into meetings: deep research, account-specific messaging, and real personalization. I studied Computer Science and built the AI infrastructure to deliver that quality at scale. Not ChatGPT with prompts. Multi-stage workflows trained on real conversion data.

The first version was built for MaiaEdge, a data center connectivity platform. It delivered 10 to 15 percent reply rates, ten times the industry average. What took 40+ hours of manual work per trade show dropped to 2 hours. MaiaEdge stopped hiring SDRs and invested in more closers. When other companies asked for the same system, SpurIntel was born.
